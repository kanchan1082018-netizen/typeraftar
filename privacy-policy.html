Privacy Policy

At TypeRaftar, accessible from our official website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by TypeRaftar and how we utilize it in strict compliance with digital privacy standards and Google AdSense Publisher Policies.

1. Zero-Keystroke Logging & Client-Side Privacy

TypeRaftar operates primarily as a client-side web utility. When you take typing exercises in English, Hindi Kruti Dev, or Hindi Unicode Mangal, all typing diagnostics, keystroke monitoring, words-per-minute (WPM) calculations, and penalty evaluations are processed directly inside your local web browser. We do not intercept, log, record, or transmit your typed keystrokes or practice texts to any external database or third-party servers.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, specifically known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to TypeRaftar and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard web platforms, TypeRaftar follows standard log file procedures. These logs record visitors when they load pages. Information collected includes IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), referring/exit pages, and timestamp data. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact Us page.
